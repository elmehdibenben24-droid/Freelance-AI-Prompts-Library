Freelance AI Prompts Hub

Premium ChatGPT & Claude Prompts for Freelancers

Client Acquisition (Niche Discovery)

I am a beginner [Insert your field, e.g., Graphic Designer/Programmer]. Analyze the Upwork platform and give me 5 sub-niches with high demand and low competition. Suggest how to build my portfolio to attract clients interested in these niches.

Proposal Writing (Cover Letter)

Here is a project description I found on Upwork: [Insert description]. Write a Cover Letter for me consisting of 4 paragraphs, focusing on the solution I will provide to the client rather than my skills, and ending with a smart question that prompts them to reply.

Task Execution (Action Plan)

The client asked me to create an action plan for a [Insert project type] project. Act as an expert in this field and give me a 5-step action plan, with key ideas and an estimated timeline to present as an initial draft to show my professionalism.

Client Communication (Scope Creep)

The client asked for many changes outside our initial agreement. Write a professional and diplomatic message explaining that these changes will require an additional budget, without ruining the relationship or sounding aggressive.
